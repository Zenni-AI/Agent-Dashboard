Soft Washing

Low-pressure cleaning that kills the algae and mildew growing on your siding and roof instead of blasting it off — and blasting your siding with it.

Get a free estimate 609-379-1760

The black streaks on your siding and the dark patches on your roof are living organisms. High pressure knocks the top layer off so it looks clean for a few weeks, then it grows straight back — and in the meantime it has driven water behind your siding and stripped granules off your shingles.

Soft washing does the opposite. Low pressure, professional cleaning solutions, and enough dwell time to kill the growth at the root. The house comes clean and it stays clean far longer.

What is included

  • Safe on vinyl, stucco, cedar, brick and painted surfaces
  • Kills algae, mildew and lichen at the root, not just the surface
  • Roof-safe — no shingle damage, no lost granules
  • Plants and landscaping protected throughout

Not sure this is what you need? Call 609-379-1760 and describe what you are looking at. If a different service is the right answer — or if it genuinely does not need doing yet — we will tell you that.

How we do it

  1. Protect first. Plants, beds and anything sensitive get wet down and covered before a drop of solution is applied. Windows and doors get checked.
  2. Apply and dwell. A cleaning solution matched to your surface is applied at low pressure and given time to work through the organic growth.
  3. Rinse. A gentle rinse carries everything off. No gouging, no lines in the stucco, no water forced under the siding.
  4. Walk it with you. We go around the house with you before we pack up, so anything missed gets handled while we are still there.

Why it matters

It actually lasts

Killing the organism rather than knocking it off is the difference between results that hold for years and results that hold for weeks.

It will not wreck your house

High pressure on vinyl, stucco or shingles causes real damage that costs real money. Soft washing is the method manufacturers actually approve.

Roofs included

A roof soft wash removes the black streaks caused by gloeocapsa magma without a single person walking your shingles more than necessary.

Common questions

Is soft washing safe for my plants?

Yes, when it is done properly. Every bed gets saturated before we start and rinsed after we finish, which dilutes anything that reaches it. This is standard on every job.

How is this different from pressure washing?

Pressure washing uses force. Soft washing uses chemistry at low pressure. Hard surfaces like concrete want pressure; siding and roofs want soft washing. Using the wrong one is how homes get damaged.

How often should I have my house washed?

Most South Jersey homes look best on a yearly cycle. Shaded, north-facing and tree-covered walls grow back faster and may want it more often.

Will it remove the black streaks on my roof?

Yes. Those streaks are algae, and a proper roof soft wash removes them without pressure and without damaging the shingles.
