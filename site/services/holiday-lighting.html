Holiday Lighting

Commercial-grade Christmas lights, custom cut to your roofline, installed and maintained by us — and taken down and stored in January so you never touch a ladder.

Get a free estimate 609-379-1760

Holiday lighting is the same skill set as everything else we do: working safely at height on your roofline, and caring what it looks like when it is finished. The season we spend on ladders in December is the reason you do not have to.

It is a full-service program, not a drop-off. We design it, we own the lights, we cut them to fit your house exactly, we come back if a run goes out, and we take it all down and store it in January.

What is included

  • Commercial-grade LEDs, custom cut to your roofline
  • Design, install, mid-season service, takedown and storage
  • Roofline, walkways, columns, trees and wreaths
  • Nothing stapled or nailed — clips only, no roof damage

Not sure this is what you need? Call 609-379-1760 and describe what you are looking at. If a different service is the right answer — or if it genuinely does not need doing yet — we will tell you that.

How we do it

  1. Design. We walk the property with you, measure the rooflines and agree on exactly what gets lit — and what it will cost — before anything is ordered.
  2. Custom cut. Commercial-grade LED runs are cut to your roofline. No gaps at the corner, no extra loop tucked behind a gutter.
  3. Install. Everything is hung on clips. Nothing is stapled, nailed or screwed into your house. Timers set, cords hidden, done before the season starts.
  4. Service and remove. If a section goes out, we come fix it. In January we take it all down, box it and store it until next year.

Why it matters

Nobody in your family on a ladder

December roofs are wet, cold and icy. This is the single most common way homeowners get hurt this time of year.

It looks intentional

Cut-to-fit runs on clean lines are the reason professionally lit houses look different from the ones with a string from the hardware store.

Zero storage, zero untangling

The lights are ours. They leave with us in January and come back working in November.

Common questions

When should I book holiday lighting?

Early. Install slots fill through the fall and the best dates go first. Reach out in late summer or early fall to lock in a date for the coming season.

Do I own the lights or do you?

We do. That is what lets us guarantee they work, service them mid-season and store them for you. It also means you are never stuck with a box of dead lights in the garage.

Will the installation damage my roof or gutters?

No. Everything hangs on clips designed for shingles and gutters. We do not staple, nail or screw anything into your house.

What if a section stops working?

Call us and we come out and fix it. Mid-season service is included — a display that goes dark halfway through December is not a display.
