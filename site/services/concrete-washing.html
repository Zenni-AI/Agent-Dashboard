Concrete Washing

Surface-cleaned concrete with an even, streak-free finish — no zebra stripes, no wand marks, no missed edges.

Get a free estimate 609-379-1760

Concrete is the first thing anyone sees and the easiest thing to get wrong. A wand in untrained hands leaves stripes across a driveway that never come out, and the difference between a clean driveway and a striped one is entirely method.

We use a rotary surface cleaner that covers the slab at a consistent distance and pressure, then hand-detail the edges, expansion joints and anything the machine cannot reach.

What is included

  • Rotary surface cleaner for an even finish edge to edge
  • Hot water where it is needed to break down grease and oil
  • Rust, oil, mildew and leaf staining treated directly
  • Optional concrete sealing to keep it clean longer

Not sure this is what you need? Call 609-379-1760 and describe what you are looking at. If a different service is the right answer — or if it genuinely does not need doing yet — we will tell you that.

How we do it

  1. Pre-treat. Organic growth, oil and rust are treated before the machine touches the slab, so the pressure is doing less of the work.
  2. Surface clean. A rotary cleaner passes the whole slab at even pressure. That is what gives you a uniform finish instead of stripes.
  3. Detail the edges. Edges, expansion joints, steps and anything up against the house are cleaned by hand to match the field.
  4. Rinse and inspect. Everything is rinsed down and walked with you. Optional sealing gets scheduled once the slab is fully dry.

Why it matters

No stripes

The single most common complaint about a cheap concrete job. A surface cleaner used correctly eliminates it.

Oil and rust come out

Vehicle oil, irrigation rust and fertilizer stains are chemistry problems. They get treated, not just sprayed at.

Safer to walk on

Algae and mildew on concrete are genuinely slippery when wet. Cleaning it is a safety improvement, not just a cosmetic one.

Common questions

Will pressure washing damage my concrete?

Not when it is done at the right pressure with the right equipment. Damage comes from holding a narrow tip too close for too long — which is exactly what a surface cleaner prevents.

Can you get the oil stains out of my driveway?

Most of them, yes. Fresh oil comes out readily; older stains that have soaked deep into the slab usually lighten substantially. We will give you a realistic answer when we look at it.

Should I seal my concrete after cleaning?

It is worth it on a driveway or a pool deck. Sealed concrete resists oil, road salt and staining, and it stays looking clean noticeably longer between washes.

How long until I can drive on it?

Right away after a straight wash, once it is dry. If you have it sealed, we will give you a specific cure time before the job — usually 24 hours before vehicles.
