Seal Coating

A protective coat over clean asphalt that shuts out water, oil and UV — the single cheapest way to add years to a driveway.

Get a free estimate 609-379-1760

Asphalt fails from the top down. Water gets into the surface, freezes, and opens it up a little more every winter. UV dries out the binder until the surface goes grey and starts to ravel. Oil softens it from the other direction.

Seal coating puts a barrier between your driveway and all three. Done on a properly prepared surface every few years, it is the difference between resurfacing and repaving.

What is included

  • Full clean and prep before any sealer is applied
  • Cracks filled and oil spots primed so the coat bonds
  • Cut-in edges around garages, walks and beds
  • Even, uniform finish on residential drives and commercial lots

Not sure this is what you need? Call 609-379-1760 and describe what you are looking at. If a different service is the right answer — or if it genuinely does not need doing yet — we will tell you that.

How we do it

  1. Clean and prep. The whole surface is cleaned, edges are cut back and every bit of loose material is blown off. Sealer will not bond to a dirty driveway.
  2. Fill the cracks. Cracks get filled and oil spots get primed. This is the step that separates a seal job that lasts from one that peels.
  3. Cut the edges. Edges against the garage, walkways and beds are cut in by hand for a clean line before the body of the drive is coated.
  4. Apply and cure. An even coat goes down across the surface. We barricade it and give you a clear cure window before you walk or drive on it.

Why it matters

Water is the enemy

Sealed asphalt sheds water instead of absorbing it. Every winter freeze-thaw cycle that cannot get inside your driveway is damage that never happens.

Slows the greying

UV breaks down the binder that holds asphalt together. A seal coat takes that hit instead of the pavement.

Cheaper than the alternative

Sealing on a regular cycle costs a fraction of resurfacing, and a small fraction of a full replacement.

Common questions

How often should a driveway be seal coated?

Every three to five years for most residential driveways. More often is not better — over-sealing builds up film that can crack and peel.

How long before I can use the driveway?

Foot traffic is usually fine after 24 hours and vehicles after 48, but it depends on temperature and humidity. We give you the exact window for your job before we start.

Can you seal a driveway with cracks in it?

We fill them first. Sealing over open cracks just hides them for a season. Anything wider than about a half inch, or areas that have started to break apart, may need patching before it makes sense to seal.

Do you do commercial lots?

Yes. Parking lots, private roads and multi-unit properties, scheduled off-hours so we are not blocking your tenants or customers.
