About us

A local company that does five things properly.

Pristine Home Services is based on S Fairview Street in Delran and works across Burlington County and the surrounding South Jersey towns.

Why the list is short

Plenty of exterior companies will tell you they do everything. What that usually means is that whoever shows up learned your particular job on the drive over.

We took the opposite approach. Five services, each with the right equipment and the right method behind it: paver restoration, soft washing, concrete washing, seal coating and holiday lighting. If you need something outside that list, we would rather point you at someone good than guess on your property.

Method over pressure

Most of the damage we get called in to fix was caused by someone pointing high pressure at a surface that could not take it — stucco gouged, vinyl siding driven full of water, shingles stripped of their granules, driveways left with stripes that never come out.

Different surfaces want different things. Concrete wants a rotary surface cleaner. Siding and roofs want low pressure and the right cleaning solution with time to work. Pavers want all of it plus sand and sealer. Knowing which is which is most of the job.

What you can expect

  • A real estimate. We look at the actual property and give you a firm written number, not a range you find out about later.
  • An honest answer. If your pavers do not need sealing yet, we will tell you that instead of selling it to you.
  • Your landscaping protected. Beds saturated before, rinsed after, and covered where it matters. Every job.
  • A walkthrough before we leave. We go around it with you. Anything you are not happy with gets handled while we are still there.

Licensed and insured. Pristine Home Services is a registered New Jersey Home Improvement Contractor and carries liability insurance. Ask and we will send you the certificate before we start — you should be asking every contractor that.
